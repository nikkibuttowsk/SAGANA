Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

SAGANA: Fruit Farms PH helps visitors find fruit farms in the Philippines and book a visit. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Who we are

SAGANA is a student project that operates this website. For anything about your data, contact us at sagana077@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you reserve a visit: your name, email address, contact number, visit date and time, and number of guests.
  • When a farm owner registers a farm: the farm and owner details you type in, such as farm name, address, admin email, entrance fee, crops, hours, description, activities and photos.
  • When you contact us: your name, email address and message.
  • When you use “Use My Location”: your approximate location, only to show nearby farms. It is used in your browser and is not saved to your reservation.
  • Technical data: basic information such as your browser type and IP address, which our hosting and map providers receive when you load the site.

How we use it

  • To process your reservation and send you a confirmation and ticket.
  • To notify the farm you booked so they can prepare for your visit.
  • To list registered farms on the site.
  • To answer your questions and improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent misuse.

Why we may process it

We process your data with your consent (for example when you submit a form), to carry out your reservation, and to meet legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, but we may then be unable to complete your reservation.

Who can see it

Your reservation details are shared with the farm you booked and with the SAGANA team. We do not sell your information. We use these service providers, who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database and file storage.
  • Email service — sends booking confirmations.
  • OpenStreetMap and Leaflet — the map; they may receive your IP address when map tiles load.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

SAGANA does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small amounts of data (for example a remembered form entry) to make the site work. You can clear this from your browser settings at any time.

How we protect it

Data is stored with access controls and sent over secure connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep reservation records only as long as needed to run the service and meet any legal obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about, access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, and claim compensation for damage caused by misuse. To make a request, email us at the address below. If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Children

SAGANA is not meant for children under 13 to use on their own. A parent or guardian should make reservations for minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this section shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: sagana077@gmail.com.

Last updated: November 2026

By using SAGANA: Fruit Farms PH you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

About the service

SAGANA lists fruit farms and lets you request a visit. The farms are run by their owners. We provide the listing and booking tool, and we are not the operator of any farm.

Reservations

  • Give accurate details so the farm can contact you.
  • A reservation depends on the farm’s availability and its own rules, such as hours, capacity and seasonal harvests.
  • Entrance fees and activities are set by each farm and may change. Please check the farm’s page before you go.
  • Cancellations and refunds are handled by the farm according to its own policy.

Farm owners

If you register a farm, you confirm that the information you submit is true and that you have the right to share the photos and details you upload. Listings may be edited or removed if they are inaccurate, misleading or against these terms.

Acceptable use

  • Do not misuse the site, attempt to break into it, or interfere with how it works.
  • Do not submit false, offensive or harmful content, or use another person’s details without permission.
  • Do not copy or scrape the site in bulk without our written permission.
  • Follow each farm’s safety instructions during your visit.

Content and ownership

The SAGANA name, logo and design belong to the SAGANA team. Farm photos and descriptions belong to the farms or their owners, who allow us to display them on the site. Please do not reuse them without permission.

Third-party links and maps

The site may link to other websites and uses map services such as OpenStreetMap. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or policies.

Accuracy and liability

We try to keep information correct, but harvest seasons, weather and availability can change. The site is provided “as is”, and to the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for changes, closures, injuries or losses that happen at or because of a farm visit.
